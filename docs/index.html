Why No AI?

This isn't anti-technology. It's pro-efficiency.

Water

A single ChatGPT conversation uses roughly 500 mL of water for datacenter cooling. Training GPT-4 consumed an estimated 700,000 liters. This tool uses zero — it runs on your machine.

Energy

An AI-powered job matching query hits GPU clusters drawing hundreds of watts. Our matching engine runs in a browser tab using milliwatts of your existing CPU. Same result, a million times less power.

Privacy

AI tools send your resume to remote servers. Your data gets logged, stored, and potentially used for training. Here, nothing leaves your browser. Not your resume, not the job description, not the results.

Cost

AI job tools charge $20-50/month. Students hunting for internships shouldn't have to pay to job search. This is free, forever — no premium tier, no "credits," no upsell.
