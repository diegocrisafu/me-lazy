I am currently a Software Developer at McKesson, building an AI agent for the AI Canada team. It ingests recorded video calls and produces structured transcripts, keyframe snapshots and generated summaries, then makes the resulting corpus queryable so the agent can retrieve context and answer questions across past meetings.

Before that, four internships across three companies. At CAE I worked in a C++ simulation codebase — LiDAR and infrared sensor pipelines, PowerShell automation that diffed Git-versioned virtual assets to surface configuration drift, and a developer productivity extension that took a recurring four-hour task down to thirty minutes.

At Presagis I prototyped an Omniverse digital-twin pipeline and worked alongside NVIDIA research on machine-learning point-cloud workflows for robotics, co-authoring three internal reports.

The thread through all of it is the same: find the manual step that keeps getting repeated, and remove it.
